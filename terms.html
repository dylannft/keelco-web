Service terms

Last updated 6 October 2026

These terms apply when a business uses Keel Co, including during a pilot. They're written in plain English. Anything agreed separately in writing with your business takes priority over these terms.

1. What Keel Co does

Keel Co provides a virtual receptionist that answers calls forwarded to your Keel Co line. It greets callers in your business's name, answers simple questions using only the information you've approved, takes a message, flags urgent matters to your on-call contacts by text message, emails summaries, and records each call in your call log.

2. What it doesn't do

  • It does not confirm, change or cancel bookings, check availability or quote prices unless you've set it up to.
  • It does not share access codes, passwords or other customers' details.
  • It does not give medical, legal or technical advice.
  • It does not promise refunds, discounts, compensation or room moves.
  • It is not an emergency service. Callers who describe an emergency are told to hang up and call 000.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Give us accurate information about your business and on-call contacts, and tell us promptly when anything changes.
  • Make sure someone is available to act on urgent alerts, and return calls within the timeframe callers are told to expect.
  • Set up and manage call forwarding on your own phone system. You can switch it off at any time.
  • Keep meeting your own obligations to your customers, including under privacy law.

4. Pilots

A pilot runs for the period we agree in writing, usually 30 days from the day your Keel Co line goes live. Before it ends we'll send you the ongoing price in writing. If you don't agree to continue, we switch the service off and you turn off call forwarding.

5. Fees

If you continue after a pilot, you pay the monthly fee we've agreed in writing. There's no lock-in contract. You can cancel by email at any time, and the service stops at the end of the month you've paid for.

6. Service availability

We work hard to keep the service running, but it relies on phone carriers and online services we don't control. Occasionally a call may not be answered, or an alert or summary may be delayed or incomplete. Keel Co is designed to reduce missed calls, not to replace your own arrangements for emergencies.

7. Mistakes

The virtual receptionist is an automated system and can occasionally mishear a name or number or misunderstand a question. Every call is saved with its transcript so you can check. If you notice a mistake, tell us and we'll correct the setup.

8. Liability

To the extent the law allows, our total liability to you for anything related to the service is limited to the fees you paid us in the month before the issue arose. We aren't liable for lost business or indirect losses. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

9. Privacy

We handle caller information as set out in our privacy policy. We use it only to deliver your messages, keep your call log and check the service is working. We never sell it or use it for marketing.

10. Ending the service

Either of us can end the service by email at any time. When it ends, turn off call forwarding. We'll disconnect your Keel Co line and delete your call records within 30 days.

11. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we'll email you at least 14 days before the change takes effect.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Queensland, Australia.
