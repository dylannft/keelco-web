Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Keel Co answers phone calls for businesses when their team can't. This policy explains what information we handle when we do that, why, and how we look after it.

Who we are

Keel Co is based in Brisbane, Queensland. You can contact us about privacy at keelco.au@gmail.com.

Whose information we handle

  • Callers: people who phone a business that uses Keel Co, such as customers, guests, patients, tenants and suppliers.
  • Clients: the businesses we work for, and the staff who use our call log.
  • Enquiries: people who contact us through this website.

What we collect from callers

When our virtual receptionist answers a call for a business, we collect:

  • the phone number the call came from
  • the caller's name and the number they'd like to be called back on
  • details relevant to their call, such as an address, a booking or an appointment
  • the reason for the call, in the caller's own words
  • a recording and written transcript of the call
  • the date, time and length of the call

Callers are told at the start of the call that they are speaking with a virtual assistant.

Why we collect it

We collect caller information to pass the caller's message to the business they called, so the business can call them back. We also review calls to check the service is working correctly and to fix mistakes.

We do not sell caller information, use it for marketing, or share it with anyone other than the business the caller rang and the service providers listed below.

Who we share it with

  • The business the caller phoned, by text message, email and through their Keel Co call log.
  • Service providers that run the system: our phone carrier (Twilio), our voice assistant and call recording provider (ElevenLabs), our automation service (n8n), our database provider (Supabase), our website host (Vercel) and our email provider (Google). They process information only to provide their service to us.

Where it's stored

Call logs are stored in our database in Sydney, Australia. Some of our service providers process or store information outside Australia, including in the United States and the European Union. We choose established providers with strong security practices and take reasonable steps to make sure information is handled consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

How long we keep it

We keep call recordings for no longer than 90 days. Call log entries are kept for as long as the business uses Keel Co, so they can look back at past calls. When a business stops using Keel Co, we delete its call records within 30 days.

Keeping it secure

Each business can see only its own calls. Staff sign in with a one-time link sent to their work email. Access to call information within Keel Co is limited to the people who run the service.

Accessing or correcting information

If you called a business that uses Keel Co and want to know what we hold about you, want it corrected, or want it deleted, email us. We'll respond within 30 days. You can also contact the business you called.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, email us first and we'll try to resolve it. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

We may update this policy as our service changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
