    What happens when a call rings out

    Your phones stay exactly as they are. Keel Co only picks up the calls your team doesn't get to.

    1. Your phone rings out

      After a set number of rings, or after hours, your phone system passes the call to your Keel Co line.

    2. Keel Co answers

      In your business's name, in a natural Australian voice. Callers are told they're speaking with a virtual assistant.

    3. It works out what matters

      Who's calling, what they need, and whether it can wait until your team is back.

    4. The right person hears about it

      Urgent calls text your on-call phone straight away. Everything else waits in your call log.

    5. Your team calls back

      With a summary and the full transcript in front of them, so nobody has to ask the caller twice.

    Ground rules it never breaks

    A receptionist that makes things up is worse than voicemail. Keel Co stays inside what your business has approved.

    • Never invents an answerPrices, availability, opening hours and policies come only from what you've given it. If it doesn't know, it says so and arranges a callback.
    • Never shares private detailsAccess codes, passwords and other customers' information stay with your team.
    • Never gives professional adviceNo medical, legal or technical advice. It takes the details and your team takes it from there.
    • Never promises what you haven'tNo refunds, discounts or callback times beyond what you've agreed.
    • Reads every number backCallback numbers and addresses are confirmed with the caller before the call ends.
    • Sends emergencies to 000Fire, gas, injury or danger: the caller is told to hang up and ring triple zero.

    Questions

    Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

    Yes. Keel Co introduces itself as a virtual assistant at the start of every call, and says so if anyone asks. Most callers just want their message to reach a person quickly, and that's what it does.

    What if it gets something wrong?

    Every call is saved with a full transcript, so your team can check exactly what was said. Names and numbers are read back to the caller before the call ends, and it never commits your business to anything it hasn't been told it can.

    Does it replace our reception team?

    No. It answers the calls that would otherwise ring out or go to voicemail: when everyone's busy, on another line, or gone home for the day.

    How are urgent calls handled after hours?

    You choose what counts as urgent and who's on call. When an urgent call comes in, the on-call person gets a text with the caller's name, the problem and their number within seconds of the call ending, plus a link to the full call.

    Where is call information kept?

    Your call log is stored in Australia, in Sydney, and only your business can see it. To answer calls, Keel Co uses established global providers for the phone line and voice, which process call audio on their own servers, some of them overseas. Our privacy policy names each one and how long information is kept.

    Can it take bookings?

    It captures booking requests in full for your team to confirm. It doesn't check availability or quote prices unless you've set it up to.
