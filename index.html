    What happens when a call rings out

    Your phone stays exactly as it is. Keel Co only steps in when you can't pick up.

    1. Your phone rings out

      If you don't answer after a few rings, your mobile forwards the call to your Keel Co line.

    2. Keel Co replies

      With text-back, the caller gets a text from your business in seconds. With AI answering, a natural Australian voice answers in your business's name, and says it's a virtual assistant.

    3. It works out what matters

      Who's calling, what they need, where the job is, and whether it can wait until you're free.

    4. You get the details

      A text to your phone with the name, number and job. Urgent ones are flagged. Everything is saved in your call log.

    5. You call back

      When you're between jobs, with everything you need in front of you. The customer is still waiting for you, not booked with someone else.

    Questions

    Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

    Yes. Keel Co introduces itself as a virtual assistant at the start of every call, and says so if anyone asks. Most callers just want their message to reach a person quickly, and that's what it does.

    What if it gets something wrong?

    Every call is saved with a full transcript, so your team can check exactly what was said. Names and numbers are read back to the caller before the call ends, and it never commits your business to anything it hasn't been told it can.

    Do I still answer my own phone?

    Yes. Your phone rings as normal. Keel Co only steps in for the calls that would otherwise ring out or go to voicemail: when you're on a job, driving, on another call, or finished for the day.

    What happens after the free trial?

    Before the 14 days are up, we'll check in and show you what it caught. If you want to keep it, you pay monthly from then on. If not, switch call forwarding off and that's it. There's no setup fee and nothing to cancel.

    Is there a contract?

    No lock-in. You pay month to month and can stop any time by letting us know. The service stops at the end of the month you've paid for.

    Does text-back work for landline callers?

    Texts can only go to mobiles. If a landline or private number calls and you miss it, you get a text with their number so you can call back.

    How are urgent calls handled?

    You choose what counts as urgent, like a termite report with a deadline or a customer who's been stung. When one comes in, you get a text with the caller's name, the problem and their number within seconds, plus a link to the full call. With AI answering, it can also put the caller straight through to you.

    Where is call information kept?

    Your call log is stored in Australia, in Sydney, and only your business can see it. To answer calls, Keel Co uses established global providers for the phone line and voice, which process call audio on their own servers, some of them overseas. Our privacy policy names each one and how long information is kept.

    Can it take bookings?

    It captures booking requests in full for your team to confirm. It doesn't check availability or quote prices unless you've set it up to.
