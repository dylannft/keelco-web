What happens when a call rings out

Your phones stay exactly as they are. Keel Co only picks up the calls your team doesn't get to.

  1. Your phone rings out

    After a set number of rings, or after hours, your phone system passes the call to your Keel Co line.

  2. Keel Co answers

    In your business's name, in a natural Australian voice. Callers are told they're speaking with a virtual assistant.

  3. It works out what matters

    Who's calling, where they're staying, what's wrong, and whether it can wait until morning.

  4. The right person hears about it

    Urgent calls text your on-call phone straight away. Everything else is emailed as a short summary.

  5. It's in your call log

    Every call is saved with a summary and full transcript, so whoever calls back has the whole story.

Ground rules it never breaks

A receptionist that makes things up is worse than voicemail. Keel Co is set up to stay inside what your business has approved.

  • Never invents an answerCheck-in times, prices, availability and policies come only from what you've given it. If it doesn't know, it says so and arranges a callback.
  • Never shares access codesDoor codes, key-safe codes and wifi passwords stay with your team, who confirm the guest first.
  • Never promises what you haven'tNo refunds, discounts, room moves or callback times beyond what you've agreed.
  • Reads every number backCallback numbers and unit numbers are confirmed with the caller before the call ends.
  • Sends emergencies to 000Smoke, gas, injury or danger: the caller is told to hang up and ring triple zero.

Questions

Will guests know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. Keel Co introduces itself as a virtual assistant at the start of every call, and says so if anyone asks. Most callers just want their problem passed to a person quickly, and that's what it does.

What if it gets something wrong?

Every call is saved with a full transcript, so your team can check exactly what was said. Names and numbers are read back to the caller before the call ends, and it never commits your business to anything it hasn't been told it can.

Does it replace our reception team?

No. It answers the calls that would otherwise ring out or go to voicemail: when everyone's busy, on another line, or gone home for the night.

How are urgent calls handled after hours?

You choose what counts as urgent and who's on call. When an urgent call comes in, the on-call person gets a text with the guest's name, location, problem and callback number within seconds of the call ending.

Where is call information stored?

Your call log is stored in Sydney. Some of the services that run the calls process information overseas. Our privacy policy lists each one and how long information is kept.

Can it take bookings?

It captures booking enquiries in full (dates, guests and preferences) for your reservations team to confirm. It doesn't check availability or quote prices unless you've set it up to.
